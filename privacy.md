Last updated November 5, 2026.

This personal academic website is maintained by Yuyang Wang. Public pages can be read without creating an account. The site currently has no advertising, Google Analytics, or Matomo integration. Its built-in search runs in your browser.

## Notice cookie

When you select **Got it**, the theme stores a cookie named `hy--cookies-ok` to remember that you dismissed the notice. It applies across this website and expires after 356 days. You can remove it through your browser's site-data settings to see the notice again. If cookies are blocked, the notice may appear again on a later visit.

The button records your acknowledgement. External fonts and libraries load when pages open, including before the notice is dismissed.

## Hosting and external resources

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and the jQuery library from Google Hosted Libraries. Publication lists also load the BibTeX display library from jsDelivr. Downloading these resources connects your browser to the relevant provider and sends connection information such as your IP address and browser details. The providers' practices are described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [jsDelivr Privacy Policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy).

Your browser may cache downloaded files. Browser settings or extensions can block external resources, which may affect fonts, interactive features, or publication formatting.
